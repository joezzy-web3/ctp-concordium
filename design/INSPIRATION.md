# UI inspiration (collected 19 Nov 2026)

Structure first; this is for the UI pass later. Pictures are in `inspo/`.

| Ref | What we take from it |
|---|---|
| 01 Hero + floating cards (dark city, white rounded cards) | Landing hero: big two-line headline left, floating white cards right. Our cards: "Agent #1287 · Verified by Concordium" and a live "Last decision: CLEARED / HELD / REFUSED" card with a 1/4 pager. |
| 02 Dashboard (dark sidebar, soft pastel stat tiles, rounded cards) | Operator dashboard: tiles for Cleared / Held / Refused this week, a bar chart of checks per day, a "Recent decisions" list (agent, verdict chip, amount, time) with each row opening its receipt. |
| 03 Portals video (black, glowing purple rings, coins on a 3D track) | Motion for the hero: an invoice coin travelling down the track and passing through the verification ring; it only continues when the ring glows green (CLEARED), stops amber (HELD) or bounces red (REFUSED). Tells the "verify before you pay" story with no words. |

Notes
- Keep the Clear to Pay identity from the Jira app (navy and gold, the flag), so both hackathon entries look like one product.
- Verdict colours are fixed meanings everywhere: green CLEARED, amber HELD, red REFUSED.
- Respect reduced-motion: the hero falls back to a still frame.
