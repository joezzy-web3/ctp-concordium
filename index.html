How a payment clears

Four checks, in this order. The contract runs them itself; the AI can read and ask, but it can never pay on its own.

  1. The agent reads the invoiceAgent #1287 submits it to the escrow. Its Concordium badge is checked first.
  2. Two people count, blindA receiver and an approver each commit a count before either can see the other’s.
  3. The supplier approves the amountFrom its own account, for this exact invoice. Nobody can pay it less.
  4. The contract paysOnly to the supplier’s own account, and only after a new account has waited 72 hours.
